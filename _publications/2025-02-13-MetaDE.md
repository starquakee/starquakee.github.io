Differential Evolution (DE) is a simple and effective method for challenging black-box optimization problems, but its performance is sensitive to the mutation factor, crossover probability, and choice of DE strategy. Parameter tuning and adaptive mechanisms address this sensitivity, yet finding effective configurations for a specific problem remains difficult.

MetaDE uses DE at a meta-level to evolve DE's own hyperparameters and strategies. Its parameterization technique changes these parameters and strategies dynamically during evolution. A GPU-accelerated implementation evaluates configurations in parallel, allowing DE to solve the target problem while optimizing its own configuration in one automated workflow.

MetaDE was evaluated on the CEC2022 benchmark suite and applied to robot control through evolutionary reinforcement learning. The [MetaDE source code](https://github.com/EMI-Group/metade) is publicly available.
